2. What the Plugin does

The Plugin produces a diagnostic of your inbound acquisition funnel and a modelled estimate of the revenue opportunity in closing the gaps it finds. Specifically it:

  • Scores six categories of funnel performance: inquiry volume, conversion rate,

follow-up discipline, response speed, offer clarity, and staff capacity.

  • Identifies your primary leak and ranks the rest by estimated cost of delay.
  • Produces a modeled opportunity estimate and a revenue range.
  • Can draft offer language and produce a blank tracking spreadsheet for your sales team.
  • Can score a prospect against the same model.
  • Can submit a request for a strategy call.

What it does not do. The Plugin does not implement changes for you, does not run your marketing, does not place advertisements, and does not contact anyone on your behalf without a separate engagement. A score is an estimate produced from the inputs you supply and the model we have built. It is not a measurement of your actual revenue and it is not a promise of what will happen if you act on it.
