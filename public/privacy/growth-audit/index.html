2. What we collect, and what we do with it

2.1 Business inputs — processed, never stored

When you run an audit, the plugin receives: your industry, monthly inbound inquiry volume, what share of inquiries become a customer, your average value per booked customer, and self-ratings for response speed, follow-up discipline, offer clarity, staff overload, and data protection, plus a few yes/no facts about your current setup.

These are held in memory for the duration of the request only. They are used to compute scores and a modeled estimate. They are not written to disk, not logged, and not transmitted to us.

This matters more than it might seem. Inquiry volume, conversion rate, and revenue per customer describe your business in detail. We deliberately treat them as transient.

2.2 Derived funnel scores — stored only with your consent

The system may store the derived results: your overall score, the six leak scores and their bands, your primary leak, and the conservative opportunity estimate.

This happens only after you explicitly agree via the privacy consent step, and you can withdraw that consent at any time. Withdrawing stops future storage and deletes what has been collected.

2.3 Contact details — stored only when you request a strategy call

If you explicitly ask to be contacted about a strategy call, we store:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your company or practice name
  • Your industry
  • What you told us the system needs to do
  • Any optional note you write

We use these for exactly one purpose: to reply about a strategy call and scope an audit.

2.4 What we never collect

The plugin's server actively rejects requests containing any of the following, and returns an error rather than storing them:

  • Patient or client identifiers, medical record numbers (MRN), medical records, ICD codes
  • Medications, prescriptions, diagnoses
  • Legal matter numbers, case numbers
  • Payment card details, bank account and routing numbers
  • Government identifiers (including Social Security numbers)
  • API keys, passwords, MFA codes, or other authentication secrets

Free-text notes are scanned for identifiers before storage and redacted if they contain them. We also do not engage in surveillance, behavioral profiling, or tracking of your query patterns beyond what is necessary to operate the plugin.

6. Retention

  • Business inputs: not retained.
  • Derived scores: retained only with consent, maximum 180 days, then automatically purged.
  • Contact details: maximum 180 days, then automatically purged.

Retention is enforced in code: expired records are purged on every read and write, not on a schedule that could be delayed.

7. Your rights

You can, at any time:

Deletion requests are honoured immediately and cover derived scores and contact details. There is no data to delete for business inputs, because they are never stored.

To exercise any of these rights, ask the assistant in the plugin, or contact us at privacy@hivemindintelligence.com.

10. Security

Data is transmitted over TLS. The MCP server validates all input against a strict schema and rejects restricted data categories at the boundary.

Your identity is resolved at the server edge, and the identity of the caller is pseudonymous: the credential used to establish it is hashed, never stored raw. Where a caller cannot be identified, the server refuses the request rather than treating it as belonging to a shared or default account. Stored scores and any contact details are therefore partitioned per caller, so one user's audit history is never visible to another.

Where the platform provides an authenticated session, that authenticated identity is used. Where the platform does not, the server relies on the caller's own signed-in account on the platform side. The plugin does not accept a raw, unverified credential from the request as a substitute for that.

We will not launch the plugin until authorization at the server edge is enforced by our own token endpoint. We are stating this plainly rather than claiming a control we have not yet switched on.
