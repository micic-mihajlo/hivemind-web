This plugin does not receive, transmit, or store your documents. Read this policy before you paste anything.

2. What this plugin actually is

The Legal Workflows plugin is a set of instructions and templates. When you ask it to review a document, the assistant in your conversation reads the text you pasted and follows the relevant workflow. That is the entire mechanism.

The plugin has:

  • No MCP server. There is no backend of ours that your document reaches.
  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for and no identifier for you.
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no tracking pixels, no advertising SDK.
  • No storage. There is no database or file store behind this plugin.

3. What we do not collect

Because there is no server, we do not collect, and cannot collect:

  • Document contents, or any part of them
  • Identifiers, party names, signatures, or contact details inside your documents
  • Client or matter names, matter numbers, or file numbers
  • Prompts you send, or the output the assistant produces
  • Device, usage, or telemetry data of any kind

4. Where your document actually goes

When you paste a document into ChatGPT or Codex, it is transmitted to OpenAI, and handled under OpenAI's privacy policy and your agreement with them. It is processed in that platform's environment to produce a response.

We are not a party to that transmission. We do not receive it, we do not have access to it, and we cannot retrieve it.

If your document is confidential, privileged, or subject to a duty of confidence, read §5 before you paste it.

5. Confidential, privileged, and sensitive material

The workflows in this plugin are designed to help with commercially sensitive agreements, which often carry confidentiality obligations, and sometimes legal professional privilege.

We cannot offer you any privilege or confidentiality protection. Privilege is not created by a tool; it is created by the legal relationship between a lawyer and a client, and it is not conferred by pasting text into a general-purpose AI assistant.

Before you paste anything, decide whether it is appropriate to place that material in the platform at all. In particular:

  • Do not paste material subject to a contractual confidentiality obligation that

prohibits disclosure to third parties, unless the counterparty or your client has consented.

  • Do not paste privileged material into a general consumer assistant if privilege matters

to your position. Consult your own counsel.

  • Do not paste personal data belonging to individuals who are not parties to your

engagement unless you have a lawful basis to share it.

Redact names, signatures, addresses, account numbers, and anything else you do not need for the review. The workflows work on structure and clauses; they do not need party identities.

6. Content you paste into a conversation

The output of a workflow is generated in your conversation by the assistant. It is a draft work product generated from the text you supplied. It is not legal advice and it is not a substitute for a qualified lawyer reviewing the actual document.

We do not review, endorse, or adopt any output. We do not receive it.

7. What we do collect

We collect information about the plugin only in the ordinary, unavoidable sense: a valid email address if you contact us, and basic technical logs that our hosting provider records for security and abuse prevention. These logs are not linked to the contents of any conversation and contain no document text.

We collect no information about the documents you use with the plugin, because the plugin never sends us any.

8. Third-party platforms

The plugin runs inside ChatGPT and Codex, operated by OpenAI. Those platforms are governed by their own terms and privacy policies. We do not control them and are not responsible for their operation, retention, or content decisions.

OpenAI may retain conversation content under its own policies, including for abuse monitoring and, for some users, training. That is a decision made under their terms, not ours. Review OpenAI's data controls and your workspace's settings before you paste anything sensitive.

9. Children

The plugin is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Security

The plugin is distributed as static instructions and templates. There is no server of ours to attack, no database to breach, and no credential for you to manage. The security properties of your document are therefore determined by the platform you paste it into and your account settings there.

11. Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data through this plugin, there is generally nothing for us to disclose, correct, or delete. If you contact us and we hold an email address you sent us, we will delete it on request.

If your question concerns data held by OpenAI in your ChatGPT or Codex account, that is a request for them, not for us.
