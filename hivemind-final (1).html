Common questions

Before you book.

What hardware is required?

A dedicated machine that fits in your office, roughly the size of a small desktop unit. I handle procurement, configuration, and setup. You don't need to be technical. You need an ethernet port and a power outlet.

How long does installation take?

Most practices are fully live within two to three weeks from the day the hardware arrives. The first week is setup and configuration. The second is workflow training and staff onboarding.

Why not just use Copilot or Gemini with enterprise privacy settings?

Enterprise tiers give you a contract, not an architecture. Your data still leaves the premises, vendor logs still exist, and the subpoena surface remains. Those tools ask you to trust the paperwork. On-premise removes the trust requirement entirely: the data physically cannot leave, and you can verify that yourself.

What about my existing tools like Clio, Dentrix, or Eaglesoft?

Keep them. The point isn't to replace your system of record. It's to layer private AI underneath the workflows your team already runs. We map integration points during the audit, and I'll tell you honestly which systems can connect and which we work around.

Is this HIPAA-compliant?

The architecture is built for practices governed by HIPAA and bar confidentiality rules: your data never touches a third-party server, there are no vendor logs, and nothing is used for model training. Every install also includes a written AI usage policy for your staff. Compliance is a property of your whole practice, not any single product, which is exactly why the engagement starts with an audit rather than a sales pitch.

What if the audit findings don't justify moving forward?

Then I'll tell you that in writing. The audit has standalone value: you leave with a clear map of your admin leaks and your exposure points regardless of whether you hire me after.
