## 2. What the Plugin does

The Plugin is a bundle of 32 jurisdiction-neutral legal document workflows. It
generates structured work product by following a defined review, drafting, or
extraction procedure over text you supply. Specifically it can:

- Review an agreement against a defined issue checklist and report what is missing,
  off-market, or risky.
- Draft an agreement or a closing checklist from a bundled template and facts you give.
- Reduce a counterparty redline to the negotiating positions it actually contains.
- Extract key legal and commercial terms from a document.
- Extract terms across a document set into a fixed-column table.
- Compare two versions of a document clause by clause.
- Proofread a document.

**What it does not do.** The Plugin does not connect to your files, does not retrieve
your documents, does not file or serve anything, does not sign, does not track
deadlines, does not send anything on your behalf, and does not retain your documents.

## 3. Not legal advice

The Plugin is a drafting and analysis tool. Its output is **work product generated

Legal advice requires the judgment of a qualified lawyer who can consider your full

**Do not rely on the Plugin as your only review.** A qualified lawyer must review the

## 6. Accuracy, completeness, and hallucination

**The Plugin can be wrong, and it can be confidently wrong.**

Language models can misread a clause, invent a term that is not in the document, miss a
risk, overstate a risk, misattribute an obligation to the wrong party, or produce a
draft that conflicts with the source. A fluent, well-formatted output is not evidence
of correctness.

Before relying on any output, read it against the source document. Every issue the
Plugin raises should be confirmed against the actual text before it goes to a counterparty
or a decision-maker.
